Background & Philosophy

A pragmatic approach to software & ideas.

I am an independent developer based in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. I spend most of my time designing backend systems, building clean web applications, and experimenting with software architectures.

My work spans two parallel tracks: client engineering, where I deliver structured web applications and tools for businesses, and independent experimentation, where I explore ideas, build systems, and write about what I learn.

What I Currently Work On

1. Web Development & Digital Products: I build clean, semantic web interfaces using HTML5, modern CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, backed by Python services. I care deeply about editorial typography, fast load times, and interfaces that respect the reader's attention.

2. AI Systems & Retrieval (RAG): Rather than relying on ungrounded generative models, I focus on retrieval-augmented systems that ground answers strictly in verified source documents. My project AssistFlow is a direct expression of this work.

3. Offline-First Tools: I believe good software should remain functional without depending continuously on remote servers. My work on DDCET Preparation explores how local databases (SQLite with WAL mode) and bundled assets can deliver resilient learning tools.

4. Writing & Research: Through MadeForNothing, I maintain a digital laboratory where I publish essays on computing, systems, craft, and books.

How I Approach Building

I prefer understanding how things work from first principles before reaching for heavy frameworks or abstractions. When building software:

  • I keep dependencies minimal and architectures inspectable.
  • I design data schemas and database models before writing UI layers.
  • I explain what I built honestly, without pretending to have more experience than I have.
  • I value durability and clarity over short-lived trends.
