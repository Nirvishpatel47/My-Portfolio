1. The Problem & Architecture Objective

Standard AI chatbots in business environments face two recurring failures: they fabricate answers when they lack factual knowledge, and they trap frustrated customers in conversational dead-ends when human intervention is necessary.

I engineered AssistFlow to address these exact failures through a strictly grounded, multi-tenant system:

  • Zero Hallucination Tolerance: Answers are synthesized strictly from retrieved knowledge chunks stored in a vector database, accompanied by labeled document citations.
  • Intelligent Escalation: When a user signals dissatisfaction or files a formal complaint, the system captures contact credentials and triggers an asynchronous email to the business owner containing the entire conversation transcript.
  • Strict Tenant Isolation: Every database query and vector similarity search is filtered by the authenticated user_id, ensuring one client company cannot inspect another's documents.

2. End-to-End System Pipeline

The platform processes documents and serves real-time inquiries through five decoupled stages:

Step 01

Ingestion & Parsing

PyMuPDF for PDFs, python-docx for documents, openpyxl for spreadsheets, and custom brace-aware chunkers for 25+ code languages.

Step 02

Vector Indexing

Gemini Embedding 2 maps content into 3072-dimensional vectors stored in Qdrant with payload indexes on user_id and document_id.

Step 03

Hybrid Orchestration

Primary queries route to Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite. If quota or rate limits occur, requests failover automatically to Groq Llama-3.3-70B.

Step 04

State Machine FSM

Visitor state transitions in PostgreSQL: NEW → ACTIVE → HANDLING_COMPLAINT → ESCALATED.

Step 05

Widget Integration

Zero-dependency embed script validating client tokens against allowed domain origins to prevent unauthorized site embedding.

3. Document Intelligence & Semantic Chunking

Fixed-length naive chunking splits sentences arbitrarily, causing broken context in vector search. AssistFlow implements two specialized chunking approaches:

  • Dependency-Free Semantic Chunking for Prose: Detects natural paragraph and topical boundaries, preserving complete thoughts within the vector payload.
  • Brace-Aware AST Chunking for Codebases: When companies upload software repositories, functions, classes, and structs are kept intact as unified chunks, allowing the LLM to understand entire algorithms rather than fragmented syntax.
  • CPU-Bound Asynchronous Offloading: File parsing is dispatched via Python's run_in_executor to avoid blocking FastAPI's asynchronous event loop during heavy document ingestion.

4. Strict Multi-Tenant Vector Partitioning

Multi-tenancy requires ironclad separation. In Qdrant, every vector upsert and search query includes a payload filter matching the authenticated user_id:

Security Boundary: Even if two tenants upload identical document names, Qdrant payload filters enforce strict isolation at the indexing layer. PostgreSQL enforces foreign keys matching tenant identities, and passwords are encrypted using modern Argon2 hashing.

Document list queries and metadata are cached in Redis with a 600-second TTL and immediate cache invalidation upon any upload or deletion event.

7. Engineering Lessons & Insights

Building AssistFlow as a complete backend from scratch provided valuable technical lessons:

  • RAG is 80% Ingestion & Chunking: The quality of an AI answer depends entirely on the cleanliness of the extracted chunks. Investing in brace-aware parsing and semantic delimiters yielded significantly higher retrieval accuracy than tuning prompts.
  • Stateful Conversation Machines are Essential: A naive chat endpoint that treats every turn independently fails when a visitor switches from inquiring about pricing to filing a grievance. Explicit FSM states allowed predictable escalation workflows.
  • Graceful Fallbacks Prevent Downtime: Cloud AI APIs encounter transient 429 quota spikes. Implementing an automatic secondary LLM fallback made the service resilient under sustained traffic.
